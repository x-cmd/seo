# Summary

India is the purest Google market of its size anywhere — above 97% of searches on 2025 referral averages, with no local champion, no meaningful second engine and no legacy wrapper. India's domestic internet champions (Flipkart, Zomato, Paytm) won their categories as apps and portals, not as search engines, and the structural explanation is that most of India came online through inexpensive Android phones where Google is the default — with no local engine having locked in the homepage habit first, as Naver had in Korea. The two real complexities are language and device. Language splits three ways: English dominates commerce and tech queries; Hindi is the largest and fastest-growing segment, written in Devanagari but frequently transliterated into Latin script (matlab and मतलब are the same query); and a dozen-plus regional languages each carry tens of millions of speakers. The discipline is explicit language strategy with hreflang between en-IN, hi and regional versions, and no machine translation — Indic users notice, and so does Google. Device is the second constraint: the phone is often the only device, on modest data plans and low-end Android, so Core Web Vitals must be tested on exactly that hardware, light pages win, and voice search matters more than in most markets because a spoken Hindi or Tamil query is longer and more conversational than a typed one. The newest change is not an engine taking share from Google but AI products taking queries from search itself: AI Overviews roll out across English and Hindi, India is consistently one of ChatGPT's largest markets, and vernacular voice-first assistants reach users for whom typing was always the barrier. For an optimizer this means the position-zero surfaces are multiplying, being the cited source in an AI answer beats a blue link at position two, and question-shaped answers in the opening of a page win both the voice layer and the AI layer. The engine choice never changed; what a page has to look like to win did.
