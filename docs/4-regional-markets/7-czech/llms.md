# Core Content

core_features:
  - Seznam.cz has been the Czech homepage since 1996 and has kept its own web index since 2005 — not a wrapper, not a metasearch
  - It is the only national-scale independent search index inside the EU, alongside Naver and Cốc Cốc globally
  - SeznamBot crawls the open web itself; results are ranked by Seznam's own signals including S-Rank (0–10 site authority, analogous to early PageRank)
  - Seznam Webmaster Tools is the closest thing to a Czech Search Console: crawled vs indexed URLs, crawl errors, URL inspection, reindex requests; verification by meta tag or file
  - SeznamBot is unusually transparent: verify via reverse DNS to *.seznam.cz plus published machine-readable IP ranges
  - SeznamBot's JavaScript rendering is weak — client-side-only SPAs may not index; serve real HTML for navigation and core content
  - Seznam accepts IndexNow since 2022; one endpoint covers Bing and Yandex too
  - The ecosystem carries its own visibility: Firmy.cz (directory), Mapy.com, Zboží.cz (e-commerce), Sreality.cz, Seznam Zprávy, Email.cz, and Sklik (the #2 paid channel after Google Ads)
  - Czech diacritics rule: keep full marks in content, strip to ASCII in URLs, set the CMS to transliterate consistently
  - The ".cz domain boost" is correlative, not causal — content language and Czech backlinks are the actual signals

# Key Information

highlights:
  - 2025 all-device (StatCounter): Google 81.6%, Seznam 11.6%, Bing 4.1%, Yandex 1.7% (mostly panel noise), DuckDuckGo 0.5%, Yahoo! 0.4%
  - Seznam is strongest on desktop (~14.6%) and tablets (~26%), weakest on mobile
  - Seznam's share was over 40% in the late 2010s; the decline has flattened
  - Agency rule of thumb: Google ~81% in Czechia against a ~92% EU average — the gap is Seznam
  - Seznam rankings differ substantially from Google.cz on Czech commercial and local queries — track them as two engines

# Summary

The Czech Republic is the one EU market where a domestic search engine still holds a double-digit share. Seznam.cz — "list" in Czech — has been the Czech homepage since 1996 and has kept its own web index since 2005, crawled by SeznamBot and ranked by its own signals including S-Rank, a 0–10 site-authority score analogous to early PageRank. On 2025 referral averages Google holds 81.6%, Seznam 11.6% and Bing 4.1%; Seznam is strongest on desktop (~14.6%) and tablets (~26%), and an agency rule of thumb puts Google near 81% in Czechia against a 92% EU average — the gap is Seznam. Three things make it strategically different from other second-place engines: a real index of its own, a full webmaster tool (Seznam Webmaster Tools, the closest thing to a Czech Search Console, with crawled-vs-indexed reports, URL inspection and reindex requests), and unusually transparent bot verification via reverse DNS plus published IP ranges. The technical caveat is JavaScript: SeznamBot's rendering is weak, so client-side-only SPAs may not index at all — serve real HTML. The ecosystem extends past the search box into Firmy.cz (the citation layer for local search), Mapy.com, Zboží.cz for e-commerce, Sreality.cz, Seznam Zprávy, Email.cz, and Sklik, which is the number-two paid search channel in the country and a separate account from Google Ads. The language rules are the same as everywhere in this chapter: full Czech diacritics in content, ASCII-transliterated URLs set consistently by the CMS, and Czech-language backlinks — the actual signals — rather than any .cz-domain magic. Track Seznam and Google.cz as two engines; their results differ substantially on Czech commercial and local queries.
