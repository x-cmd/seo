  - Cốc Cốc maintains its own index of the Vietnamese web with its own crawler (coccocbot / coccocbot-web) — not a wrapper, not a metasearch

  - The short list of engines that crawl and rank their own national web at scale: Google, Bing, Yandex, Baidu, Naver, Seznam, Cốc Cốc

  - Bing is unusually small in Vietnam (0.7% vs ~4% global), showing Windows defaults do not carry a market on their own

# Summary

Vietnam looks like another Google-only market and mostly is — Google holds over 94% of searches on 2025 referral averages — but it hosts one of the last genuinely independent national search indexes: Cốc Cốc. The company started as a browser maker (a Chromium fork tuned for the Vietnamese web) and built a search engine inside it that crawls the Vietnamese web with its own bot (coccocbot), ranks it with its own signals, and is neither a wrapper nor a metasearch. The list of engines still crawling and ranking their own national web at scale now runs Google, Bing, Yandex, Baidu, Naver, Seznam and Cốc Cốc — everyone else licenses or merges. Cốc Cốc's share rose from roughly 3.5–4% to about 5.6% through 2025, strongest on local services, Vietnamese e-commerce, and queries typed without diacritics — its handling of unmarked Vietnamese orthography is more forgiving than Google's, and Vietnamese users frequently type nha hang rather than nhà hàng because tone-mark entry is slow. The practical content rules follow: write full, correct Vietnamese with all marks in titles, descriptions and body; transliterate URLs to ASCII consistently; and do not expect an English page to surface — Vietnamese users search in Vietnamese, and a page with no Vietnamese has nothing for the query to match. There is no Cốc Cốc submission portal comparable to Search Console; the way in is simply to be crawlable — allow coccocbot in robots.txt, and the 4–6% it holds is real traffic for the cost of one line. Bing, at 0.7% in Vietnam against a ~4% global average, is covered by whatever Bing work is done for other markets. Cốc Cốc's stated direction — AI-first search grounded in its own Vietnamese corpus — is the same independent-index-as-moat argument Seznam makes in Czech and Naver makes in Korea.
