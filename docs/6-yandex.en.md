# Yandex SEO — Russian-Language Search, Yandex.Webmaster, and Yandex.Metrica

**Yandex** is the dominant Russian-language search engine
and one of the largest tech companies in the CIS region.
Founded in 1997 and headquartered in Moscow / Amsterdam,
Yandex operates its own crawler (YandexBot), its own index,
its own webmaster tool (Yandex.Webmaster), its own ad
platform (Yandex.Direct), and its own analytics
(Yandex.Metrica).

This article is a **skeleton / placeholder** for Yandex SEO
— basic positioning, official URLs, and the surface that
Yandex SEOs touch. A full article with detailed ranking
factors and step-by-step optimization guidance will follow in
a separate PR.

> **TL;DR.** Yandex SEO = Russian-language content +
> Yandex.Webmaster verification + Yandex.Metrica
> installation. Yandex weighs behavioral signals
> (Metrica-derived) and geo-targeting more than Google. No
> ICP-style filing required.

## Why Yandex?

- **~60% of Russian-language search market** in 2026.
- **Default search engine** in Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan,
  and across the CIS region.
- **Yandex ecosystem** — Yandex.Search, Yandex.Webmaster,
  Yandex.Metrica, Yandex.Direct (ads), Yandex.Zen / Pulse
  (content), Yandex.Maps, Yandex.Taxi.
- **YandexGPT** — Yandex's large language model, integrated
  into search as an AI overview surface.

For audiences in Russia / CIS, Yandex is the default
search engine. Google has a much smaller share in the
region.

## How Yandex works

- **Crawler** — YandexBot (also YandexImages, YandexMedia,
  etc.).
- **Index** — independent of Google / Bing.
- **NLP** — strong Russian morphology, language-specific
  ranking signals.

## Yandex vs Google — what's different

Skeleton highlights (full comparison to follow):

- **Behavioral signals matter more.** Yandex.Metrica data
  (bounce rate, time on site, page depth) feeds back into
  ranking. Installing Metrica is a SEO-relevant action.
- **Geo-targeting is heavier.** Yandex weighs host IP
  geo-location and `.ru` / `.рф` TLDs more strongly than
  Google.
- **No ICP-style filing.** Unlike Baidu / Shenma, Yandex
  does not require government filing for ranking.
- **Russian-language content preferred.** For Russian
  audiences, Russian-language content ranks higher.

## How to optimize for Yandex (skeleton)

- **Russian-language content** for Russian audiences.
- **Verify site in Yandex.Webmaster** (webmaster.yandex.com)
  — submit sitemap.
- **Install Yandex.Metrica** for behavioral ranking signals.
- **Use HTTPS** (required since 2018).
- **Build backlinks from `.ru` / `.рф` domains.**
- **Allow YandexBot in robots.txt.**

## Yandex-specific ranking factors (skeleton)

- **Content quality** — unique Russian-language content.
- **Geo / host location** — host IP matters for geo queries.
- **Behavioral signals** — Yandex.Metrica engagement data.
- **Backlinks** — `.ru` / `.рф` domains weighted.
- **HTTPS / mobile / page speed** — baseline.

## Yandex AI features (skeleton)

- **YandexGPT** — Yandex's LLM. Powers AI-overview-style
  answers at the top of some queries. Same pattern as
  Google AI Overviews / Bing Copilot.
- Cite high-quality Russian-language sources to appear in
  AI-overview citations.

## Common Yandex SEO mistakes (skeleton)

- **Non-Russian content for Russian audience.**
- **Yandex.Webmaster not verified** — sitemap not submitted.
- **Yandex.Metrica not installed** — missing behavioral
  signals.
- **Blocking YandexBot** in `robots.txt`.

## Quick Yandex checklist (skeleton)

- [ ] Russian-language content for Russian audiences.
- [ ] Site verified in Yandex.Webmaster.
- [ ] XML sitemap submitted.
- [ ] Yandex.Metrica installed on all pages.
- [ ] HTTPS enforced.
- [ ] YandexBot allowed in `robots.txt`.
- [ ] `.ru` / `.рф` backlinks from relevant domains.

## Yandex vs Google — at a glance (skeleton)

| Dimension | Yandex | Google |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Index | Yandex's | Google's |
| Crawler | YandexBot | Googlebot |
| Webmaster tool | Yandex.Webmaster | Google Search Console |

| AI feature | YandexGPT | AI Overviews |
