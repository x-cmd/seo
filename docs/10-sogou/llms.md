# Summary

Sogou SEO in 2026 = Simplified Chinese content + ICP filing (if mainland-hosted) + Tencent ecosystem presence (WeChat 公众号 / QQ.com). Sogou is a top-3 Chinese-language search engine, owned by Tencent since 2021. Sogou directly indexes WeChat 公众号 articles — an active 公众号 can rank on Sogou without traditional website backlinks, which is its unique edge vs Baidu. ICP filing is recommended for mainland-hosted sites (same as Baidu; Apple App Store in China also requires it). Sogou's webmaster tool is at zhanzhang.sogou.com (Sogou Search Console) — verify site, submit sitemap + URLs. Sogou Input, the most popular Chinese IME, feeds Chinese-language query data into Sogou Search. The AI-overview surface on Sogou is lighter than Baidu AI 摘要 / Google AI Overviews — long-form first-hand Chinese content and citation-worthy claims win. Allow Sogou Spider in robots.txt; verify in Sogou Search Console; file ICP; maintain an active WeChat 公众号; build .cn backlinks.
