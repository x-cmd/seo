# Core Content

core_features:
  - Every engine in the library in one table, with five facts per row: home, index, 2025 share, users (estimate), when it matters, how to get in
  - The Index column is the most predictive: own-index engines need their own submission and ranking work; engines on Bing's or Google's index are reached by reaching the upstream; meta-searches forward queries live
  - Only about seven own-index engines remain at scale: Google, Bing, Yandex, Baidu, Naver, Seznam, Cốc Cốc (Qwant's is small but real)
  - Wrapper families are bigger than they look — Bing's index serves DuckDuckGo, Yahoo! Search and Ecosia; Google's serves Yahoo! JAPAN, Yahoo! Taiwan and Startpage
  - IndexNow covers three of the seven front doors at once (Bing, Yandex, Seznam); plus Search Console that is four of seven, and through them most wrapper rows
  - Share column is StatCounter 2025 referral averages — undercounts engines that answer on their own pages (Naver most)
  - User estimates come from mixed published bases (app MAU, site visits, query estimates) — order-of-magnitude context only, never comparable statistics

# Key Information

highlights:
  - Naver: 45.6% of Korean referrals, 62.9% of queries; app MAU ≈ 46.9M (Jul 2026)
  - Daum: 1.2%; Daum app MAU ≈ 6.3M; survives as the `#` search inside KakaoTalk, which reaches ~90%+ of Korean users
  - Yandex 73.2% of Russia; Seznam 11.6% of Czechia (14.1% desktop), ≈214M visits/month (SimilarWeb Apr 2025)
  - Cốc Cốc 4.4% in Vietnam on the 2025 average, 5.3–6.0% in late 2025; admitted by allowing coccocbot in robots.txt
  - Yahoo! JAPAN (9.4%) and Yahoo! Taiwan (13.3%) both serve Google's index — no separate submission exists
  - A separate table covers non-engine search surfaces: TikTok search, Shopee/Lazada/Tokopedia, Mercado Livre, KakaoTalk #, LINE, WhatsApp Business

# Summary

This page is the map for the whole library: every engine with a dedicated article appears once, with the five facts that explain it — home market, index ownership, 2025 home share, a user-size estimate, when it matters for SEO, and how a site gets in. The index column carries most of the meaning: only about seven engines still crawl and rank their own index at scale (Google, Bing, Yandex, Baidu, Naver, Seznam, Cốc Cốc, plus Qwant's small one), and every other row on the page is a product built on one of those seven — Bing's index quietly serving DuckDuckGo, Yahoo! Search and Ecosia, Google's serving Yahoo! JAPAN, Yahoo! Taiwan and Startpage. That is why the "how to get in" column repeats: there are only seven front doors, and IndexNow plus Google Search Console opens four of them (Bing, Yandex, Seznam, Google), covering most wrapper rows in passing. Share figures are StatCounter 2025 referral averages, which undercount engines that answer queries on their own pages — Naver most of all, which reads 45.6% on referrals but 62.9% on Korean query volume. User numbers come from mixed published bases (app MAU, site visits, daily query estimates) and are order-of-magnitude context only. A third table lists the surfaces that take queries without being search engines at all — TikTok search, the Southeast Asian and Brazilian marketplaces, KakaoTalk's `#` search, LINE, WhatsApp Business — because in those markets a growing share of looking-things-up never touches a search box.
