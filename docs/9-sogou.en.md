# Sogou SEO — Tencent-Backed Chinese Search with WeChat & QQ Ecosystem Integration

**Sogou (搜狗)** is a major Chinese-language search engine
launched in 2004, owned by **Tencent** since 2014 (with the
remaining shares acquired in 2021). Sogou Search and the
Sogou Input Method Editor (搜狗输入法) are tightly integrated
with Tencent's products — **WeChat** (微信), **QQ**, and
**QQ.com**.

This article is a **skeleton / placeholder** for Sogou SEO
— basic positioning, official URLs, and the Sogou SEO surface.
A full article with detailed ranking factors and step-by-step
optimization guidance will follow in a separate PR.

> **TL;DR.** Sogou SEO = Chinese content + ICP filing (if
> mainland-hosted) + Tencent ecosystem presence (WeChat 公众号
> / QQ.com). Sogou Search Console (zhanzhang.sogou.com) for
> sitemap and URL submission. WeChat 公众号 articles get
> prominent placement in Sogou results.

## Why Sogou?

- **Top 3 Chinese-language search engine** in mainland China
  (after Baidu; alongside Shenma on mobile).
- **Tencent-backed.** Tight integration with WeChat 公众号,
  QQ, and QQ.com content.
- **Sogou Input** — the most popular Chinese IME, feeds
  Chinese-language query streams into Sogou Search.
- **Aggregated results.** Pulls from WeChat 公众号, QQ.com,
  and Sogou's own crawler.

For Chinese audiences and Tencent ecosystem products, Sogou
is highly relevant.

## How Sogou works

- **Crawler** — Sogou Spider (`Sogou Pic Spider /
  Sogou News Spider`).
- **Index sources** — own crawl + WeChat 公众号 articles
  + QQ.com + partner APIs.
- **NLP** — strong Simplified Chinese.

## Sogou vs Baidu — what's different

Skeleton highlights (full comparison to follow):

- **WeChat 公众号 + QQ.com presence weighs heavily.** Articles
  from active 公众号 can rank on Sogou even without a
  traditional website backlink profile.
- **Sogou Input signals.** Query data from Sogou Input feeds
  into Sogou Search's understanding of user interest.
- **ICP filing recommended** for mainland-hosted sites (same
  as Baidu).
- **Smaller market share** than Baidu (~5-15% mobile,
  varies by region).

## How to optimize for Sogou (skeleton)

- **Simplified Chinese content** for Chinese audiences.
- **File ICP** if hosting on mainland-China servers.
- **Maintain an active WeChat 公众号** with regular posts —
  articles get indexed by Sogou and surfaced in results.
- **Verify site in Sogou Search Console** (zhanzhang.sogou.com)
  — submit sitemap and individual URLs.
- **Use HTTPS** (recommended since 2017).
- **Build `.cn` backlinks.**
- **Allow Sogou Spider in robots.txt.**

## Sogou-specific ranking factors (skeleton)

- **Chinese content** — Simplified Chinese ranks higher.
- **ICP filing** — helps ranking if mainland-hosted.
- **WeChat 公众号 articles** — directly indexed, get
  visibility.
- **QQ.com presence** — links / mentions on QQ.com content.
- **`.cn` backlinks** — Chinese-domain backlinks weighted.
- **Title + meta description** — weighted heavily.

## Sogou AI features (skeleton)

- Sogou has invested in AI (including a long-time stake in
  Zhihu), but its AI-overview surface on the search results
  page is lighter than Baidu AI 摘要 / Google AI Overviews.
- For now, long-form first-hand Chinese content and citation-
  worthy claims win in any AI-overview placement.

## Common Sogou SEO mistakes (skeleton)

- **No ICP filing** for mainland-China sites.
- **No WeChat 公众号** — misses the strongest Sogou ecosystem
  signal.
- **English-only content** for a Chinese audience.
- **Sogou Search Console not verified** — sitemap not
  submitted.
- **Blocking Sogou Spider** in `robots.txt`.
